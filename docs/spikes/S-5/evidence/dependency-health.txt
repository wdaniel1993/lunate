== notes ==
ReactiveUI 26.x does not depend on System.Reactive; it ships its own
ReactiveUI.Primitives (RxVoid, ISignals, ISequencer) and Splat 21.
Mixing it with System.Reactive requires explicit interop at IObservable seams
(and Subscribe() extension calls are ambiguous - see S5.VariantBPlus).
